## Common Questions

Is ContHunt just another social listening or ad spy tool?

No. Tools like BuzzSumo/Hootsuite track mentions; ad spies show paid creatives. ContHunt is true content intelligence—it aggregates organic short-form videos
across platforms, then uses agentic AI for
frame-by-frame structural breakdown (hooks, pacing, overlays, micro-patterns) to reveal why content goes
viral and how to replicate it before saturation hits.


Which platforms are actually supported right now?

Full deep integration: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts. LinkedIn video + X (Twitter) in private
beta—rolling wider soon. One canvas, no platform-hopping.


How does agentic search actually work—and do I need to be technical?

Zero Boolean or setup. Say: "Women's activewear hooks 10–15s with >2M views, high comment-to-like ratio,
last 45 days." AI decomposes → runs smart multi-keyword combos → surfaces winners. Plain English → best
results.


How deep is the frame-by-frame AI analysis really?

Brutally deep. It indexes visuals, text timing, transitions, expressions, product reveals, sound cues—things
humans miss at scale. Chat with your board: "Show every video with a question hook in first 3s" → instant
clips + insights.


Can I organize videos and turn them into decks fast?

Yes—add to boards instantly. Boards auto-index for AI chat access (no delay). One click → high-performance
marketing deck: grouped patterns, virality deconstruct, success metrics, narrative breakdowns, ready for
team/client handoff.


What's the difference vs. tools like Pipiads, MagicBrief, or Foreplay?

They spy on ads (saturated winners). We hunt organic virality first—low-competition patterns, early momentum
signals, postmortem/premortem virality indexing—so your organic game amplifies paid ROAS instead of copying
yesterday's ads.


How many videos/searches can I actually run?

Creator: 50 searches/mo + solid analysis volume.

Pro Research: 300 searches + unlimited deep dives, priority AI credits.

Enough for heavy niche scanning without hitting walls.


Is my data secure and private?

Isolated per account. We don't train models on your boards/searches. Export everything anytime. Strict
privacy—no sharing.


Can I cancel or get a refund if it's not for me?

Monthly, no lock-in. 14-day money-back guarantee (T&Cs apply). Export boards/decks before you go.


Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. We offer monthly billing with no long-term contracts.
You can export your data before you leave.
