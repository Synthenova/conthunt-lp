# Task 16 Monitoring And Reporting Setup

Date: 2026-04-16

Defined a weekly watch list and alert thresholds for the pages most likely to move.

Weekly watch list:
- refreshed pages
- merge targets
- cluster pillars
- schema/indexing regressions

Alert thresholds:
- CTR drop of 25%
- position drop of 3 or more
- impression drop of 20%
- any schema or indexing regression alerts immediately

Reporting format:
- winners
- watch list
- schema/indexing regressions
- next actions
