**Role:** Content Creator  
**Profile:** [https://conthunt.app/alex](https://conthunt.app/alex)  
**Team:** ContHunt editorial team, Synthenova Enterprises LLP

Alex writes for people who make short-form video and need a way to study public examples without turning one viral post into a religion. The work is practical: what appears in the first seconds, what the caption promises, and whether the same pattern shows up more than once.

## Bio

Alex is a content creator with 500K+ followers across TikTok and YouTube. He breaks down what makes videos travel and teaches creators, agencies, and brand teams how to test those patterns on their own accounts. At ContHunt he focuses on format analysis, hook language, and examples that a reader can open in the original source. This is founder-adjacent creator research: first-hand review of public posts, not a case study of private analytics results.

This is not a private analytics dump from other creators. It is a method for reviewing public posts, keeping the link, and writing a brief you can actually shoot.

## What does Alex research?

- Opening hooks and the first on-screen line
- How tutorials, transformations, and list videos are structured
- Where captions repeat or contradict the video
- Which ideas are reusable versus one-off stunts
- How to turn a public example into a test, not a clone

## How should you use these articles?

Start with the question you need to answer, not with a view count. If you are looking for Reels ideas, read the ideas page first and then inspect live examples in ContHunt with the source visible. Change one variable when you publish: hook, length, or caption. Measure retention and replies on your account. If a tactic cannot be measured, leave it as a hypothesis.
