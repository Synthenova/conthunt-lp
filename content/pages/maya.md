**Role:** Social Media Analyst  
**Profile:** [https://conthunt.app/maya](https://conthunt.app/maya)  
**Team:** ContHunt editorial team, Synthenova Enterprises LLP

Maya tracks what is repeating across platforms and writes it down as a method. She is less interested in a single viral spike than in whether a format keeps showing up in the same niche.

## Bio

Maya analyzes social media trends for a living. She watches what is working across TikTok, Reels, and Shorts, then helps creators, agencies, and brand teams decide what is worth testing. At ContHunt that means comparing public posts with the source still attached, and refusing to treat view count as a complete explanation. Founder-led notes stay attached to the URL. This is not a case study of private analytics results.

## What does Maya research?

- Cross-platform adaptation of the same idea
- Analytics questions that native apps can and cannot answer
- Competitor tracking that stays inside public data
- How to grow a Shorts channel without copying a template blindly

## How should you use these articles?

Pair an analytics guide with a live walkthrough in the native app, then use ContHunt to inspect other people’s public posts. Write down the date range and the sample size. If two posts look similar, note what actually changed: hook, length, or offer.
