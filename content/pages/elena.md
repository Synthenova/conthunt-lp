## Bio

Elena leads marketing at ContHunt. Her background is content strategy for B2B SaaS. She helps brands tell stories that can be checked: who the page is for, what evidence it uses, and what the reader should do next. Founder-led product language from Synthenova Enterprises LLP is kept aligned with public documentation rather than slogan math.

## What does Elena research?

- How to describe a research tool without inventing features
- When a comparison page is useful versus when it is a mismatch for the query
- Policy and copyright questions that creators actually hit
- Messaging that stays consistent across blog, about, and product copy

## How should you use these articles?

Read the positioning and policy pages as constraints, not as growth hacks. If a page discusses copyrighted music, Instagram strategy, or analytics, confirm the current platform rule on the official help site before you publish. Use ContHunt to inspect public examples, then apply the editorial standard: source, date, and a test you can run.
