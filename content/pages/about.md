ContHunt is a content intelligence platform for discovering, comparing, and analyzing public short-form videos across TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and related social surfaces.

The product is built for creators, agencies, and brand teams who need a repeatable research loop: find examples, keep the original source link, inspect hooks and packaging, and turn repeating patterns into briefs they can test. It is operated by **Synthenova Enterprises LLP**, based in Chennai, India. Founder-led product work stays close to the research workflow rather than treating total views as a ranking of ideas.

## Who is ContHunt for?

- Creators who want to brief the next batch of Shorts, Reels, or TikToks from public examples instead of scrolling endlessly
- Agencies that need a shared, source-linked view of competitor and niche creative
- Brand teams that want to inspect pacing, overlays, captions, and comments before they spend production time
- SEO and content teams who use short-form research to support landing pages, blogs, and paid creative

ContHunt is not a private-analytics login for someone else's account, and it is not a guarantee that any format will go viral. It is a research workspace for public content.

## What does the product actually compare?

Typical research questions the product is designed to support:

- Which hooks appear first in a niche, and how long they last
- How on-screen text, captions, and spoken lines stay aligned
- How similar formats perform across TikTok, Reels, and Shorts
- What comments reveal about the viewer question
- Whether a high-view post is an outlier or part of a repeating pattern

Every observation should stay attached to the original URL. If a claim cannot be checked against a source or against your own analytics, treat it as a hypothesis.

## How do we handle evidence?

We prefer official platform documentation, first-party product behavior, and public examples with links. We do not invent engagement benchmarks, “algorithm leaks,” or guaranteed outcomes. When a number appears, it should be dated and sourced, or it should be described as an observation from a specific sample.
