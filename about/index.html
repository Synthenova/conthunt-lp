About ContHunt

ContHunt is an AI-powered content intelligence platform for creators, marketers, and SEO teams.

We build tools that help users:

  • discover topics with demand
  • analyze short-form content
  • compare competitors
  • track search and content performance
  • turn content ideas into repeatable workflows

ContHunt is operated by Synthenova Enterprises LLP, based in Chennai, India.

What We Focus On

  • YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels strategy
  • creator growth and short-form analysis
  • competitor analysis
  • content planning and publishing workflows
  • SEO and GEO visibility for content teams
