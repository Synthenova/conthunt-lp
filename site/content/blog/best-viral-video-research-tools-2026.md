## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best viral video research tool for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts together?

For teams publishing across all three short-form platforms, **ContHunt** (from $25/month billed yearly, with Deep Search, saved boards, and hosted MCP included) and **Virlo** (from $49/month) are purpose-built for cross-platform discovery, while **Shortimize** (from $99/month) focuses on ongoing tracking dashboards for fixed competitor lists.

### Can any viral video research tool show a competitor’s private watch time or retention curve?

No. TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube do not expose average watch duration, swipe-away rate, completion curves, Instagram DM sends, or TikTok Shop net profit to any third-party tool. Every research tool works on public counters and creative content; pair public outlier discovery with your own native TikTok Analytics, Instagram Insights, and YouTube Studio data.

### Which viral video research tools offer an MCP server for Claude Code or Cursor in 2026?

As of November 2026, **ContHunt** includes its hosted MCP server (`https://mcp.conthunt.app/mcp`) on every paid plan starting at $25/month billed yearly. **1of10** includes YouTube MCP access on its Pro plan ($69/month yearly), **Foreplay** includes MCP access for paid ads on its Basic plan ($49/month yearly), **Shortimize** offers MCP as a paid add-on on Launch/Scale (included on Enterprise), and **SortFeed** offers MCP on supported tiers.

### How do I tell if a video is a real viral outlier instead of a lucky fluke?

Measure the video’s views against the **median of that creator’s last 10 uploads** (not the average and not follower count) and look for **5×+ median** on clips under 90 days old — then verify that **2–3 unrelated creators** in the same niche are getting lift from the same angle.
