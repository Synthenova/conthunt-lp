## Profile-sorting extensions (SortFeed) vs. cross-platform research tools

SortFeed is popular because it meets creators right inside Instagram or TikTok: open one competitor profile, click sort by views or outlier score, and see their top posts without leaving the tab. That workflow is hard to beat when you already know the exact three competitors you want to inspect.

Two friction points appear once you scale beyond occasional checks:

1. **Sorting one profile at a time misses breakout small accounts.** If you only sort accounts you already follow, you never discover a 4,000-follower creator in your niche whose hook just hit 8x their normal views.
2. **Raw view sorts surface two-year-old flukes.** Sorting a full grid by all-time views brings up old audio trends instead of formats working this month. Even on a $0 workflow, comparing a creator's last 12–15 posts against their **median views** (flagging 3x–5x recent outliers) beats sorting an entire archive.

When you need to search across an entire niche on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts at once — and group winning hooks into shared boards for editors or clients — a dedicated cross-platform search tool like ContHunt or Virlo replaces opening 20 tabs by hand.
