## Why creators outgrow Social Blade

Social Blade is built around account-level totals: total subscribers, daily view deltas, and modeled earnings ranges. Three limits show up quickly once a team uses it for content planning:

1. **Totals hide which video caused the lift.** Seeing that a channel added 40,000 views on Thursday does not show whether one 28-second Short drove the spike or an old tutorial re-entered browse.
2. **Estimated earnings ranges are too wide for business decisions.** Public RPM guesses do not know a channel's audience geography, sponsorship deals, Shorts vs. long-form split, or monetization status.
3. **Short-form discovery happens at the video level, not the channel level.** On TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts, a 3,000-follower account can produce a repeatable format that outperforms a 300,000-follower account. Account-size leaderboards filter those clips out.
