Kalodata is a TikTok Shop analytics product for researching products, shops, creators, videos and livestreams. If you are comparing its price with alternatives, start with the job: a TikTok Shop sales dashboard and a cross-platform creative research tool answer different questions. Check the current price and market coverage in each product before buying.

## How much does Kalodata cost?

We could not independently verify a current public plan price from [Kalodata's pricing page](https://www.kalodata.com/pricing) on 22 November 2026. Its [homepage](https://www.kalodata.com/) advertises a seven-day trial. Check the offer shown to your account, including billing period, country coverage, seats, historical access and cancellation terms, before treating any quoted monthly figure as your cost.

A monthly equivalent billed annually is not the amount charged each month. Multiply it by twelve and check the actual checkout total, renewal date and tax. Also check whether the market you need is included in the plan you choose.

## Which Kalodata alternative fits the job?

| Tool | Job to investigate | Where it differs |

| [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/) | Tracking public short-form accounts and videos | Built around short-form tracking rather than a TikTok Shop product database |
| [ContHunt](/pricing) | Researching public creative across TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts | It is not a replacement for a TikTok Shop sales database |

For a seller choosing products or creators by TikTok Shop data, compare Kalodata and FastMoss on the same products, market and dates. Ask each vendor how its figures are sourced, whether sales are estimates, how history is limited and what the plan includes. A higher reported number does not establish which estimate is more accurate.

For a creative team asking how products are presented in public videos across platforms, ContHunt serves a different part of the workflow. Use it to collect examples and examine hooks or formats; use the relevant commerce dashboard and your own shop data for sales decisions. Public video metrics cannot reveal another shop's private profitability or conversion rate.

## What should a TikTok Shop team test before subscribing?

1. Choose one market and a handful of products you already understand. Search the same examples in each trial.
2. Record which sellers, creators, videos and dates each product returns. Check whether the missing results matter to your decision.
3. Compare historical windows, data refresh, exports and seats at the price you would actually pay.
4. Label estimates as estimates. Compare any vendor figure with first-party shop data where you have access.
5. Decide whether you also need creative research on Reels and Shorts, rather than assuming a commerce dashboard covers every platform.
