## 2. Kalodata

**Best for:** TikTok Shop sellers, dropshippers, and affiliate agencies researching estimated product revenue, shop leaderboards, top-converting affiliates, and livestream sales.

**Pricing (Nov 2026):** 7-day free trial (10 searches/day, 30-day history); US Starter **$59.90/mo** (~$49.92/mo billed annually; 50 searches/day, 90-day history); Professional **$119.90/mo** (~$99.92/mo annually; 250 searches/day, 180-day history, creator contacts & exports); Enterprise custom. Kalodata requires login for checkout pricing and localizes rates outside the US.

**Honest caveats:** Strictly TikTok Shop; all GMV and unit-sales figures are modeled estimates, never exact first-party Seller Center payouts. See our [Kalodata pricing & alternatives guide](/blog/kalodata-pricing-alternatives) for a fuller evaluation checklist.
