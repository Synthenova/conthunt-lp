The best TikTok hashtags describe the subject and audience of the video you actually made. There is no verified set of hashtags that guarantees a viral post. Pick relevant tags, inspect the videos behind them and review your own results alongside the opening, topic and audience response.

## Do hashtags make a TikTok go viral?

Hashtags are one signal, not a guarantee. [TikTok's explanation of recommendations](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content) lists hashtags among content information for the For You feed and says user interactions, including time spent watching, are generally weighted more heavily for most users. Search also considers how well content matches a query, along with other signals. Those statements describe factors, not a formula for an individual video.

A relevant hashtag can help describe a topic. It cannot fix a video whose opening does not deliver on its promise, or prove that a tag caused a change in views. Look at the whole package before changing a caption.

## How do you find relevant hashtags?

Start with the subject in plain language. Search that phrase on TikTok and open several videos that actually answer the same question. Record the recurring terms and tags, but also note the opening and the audience being addressed. A tag on an unrelated popular clip is not necessarily relevant to yours.

[TikTok Creative Center's Trends guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-trends) describes filters for industry and time frame and hashtag analytics including regional popularity, related videos and trendlines. Use those as research clues: check the region and dates before adopting a trend, then watch the related posts to see whether the topic fits.

You can also [browse public short-form examples by niche](/viral-now). Treat them as examples to investigate, not proof that their tags caused their performance.

## How many hashtags should you use?

Use only tags that add useful context. TikTok for Business has [recommended five relevant hashtags](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/GrowwithTikTok_FAQ_ENG2.pdf) in a business FAQ; that is a business recommendation, not a universal threshold or a guarantee for organic posts. If you use fewer, make each one relevant. Avoid padding a caption with unrelated trending tags just to fill a quota.

For an illustrative home-cooking video about a quick bean lunch, a creator might test a topic tag, a dish-specific tag and a broader cooking-community tag. Check those tags in your own language and market first; a plausible phrase in this example is not evidence that it is popular.

## How should you test a hashtag change?

1. Record the topic, opening, caption, tags, posting time and video length for a group of comparable posts.
2. Change one reasonable element at a time where possible, and keep the audience and topic similar.
3. Review your own analytics at the same age after posting. Note traffic sources and viewer response as well as views.
4. Repeat the comparison. A single spike can have several causes, so do not credit the hashtag alone.
5. Keep a record of the examples and the decision you make for the next video.
