## How to avoid chasing fake Shorts outliers

Before you script any YouTube Short based on a research tool, apply two filters:

1. **Score against the channel’s 10-video median (never the mean or subscriber count).** On Shorts, one 300,000-view clip pulls a 10-video average from 1,500 up to 31,000 and hides real winners. Bookmark **5×+ median** clips under 90 days old — and check your math in our [Viral Outlier Score Calculator](/tools/viral-outlier-score-calculator).
2. **Respect Shorts’ lower like-to-view baseline.** Do not reject a winning Shorts hook just because its public like-to-view is 2.2% instead of 5.5%. In our [2026 benchmark of 500 YouTube Shorts](/blog/short-form-video-engagement-benchmark-2026), **1.77%** was the median and **3.12%** was already the 75th percentile.
