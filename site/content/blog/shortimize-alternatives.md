Shortimize is designed for tracking short-form videos and accounts. The right alternative depends on the gap: use native analytics for your own private audience and retention data, a tracking platform for a recurring public account watchlist, and ContHunt for researching public video creative across platforms. These jobs overlap, but they are not identical.

## What does Shortimize cost?

On 22 November 2026, [Shortimize's pricing page](https://www.shortimize.com/pricing) listed Launch at $99 a month and Scale at $249 a month in its monthly view. Launch listed one seat, 1,000 tracked videos and a 24-hour refresh; Scale listed three seats, 5,000 videos and a 12-hour refresh. Its page listed API, webhooks and MCP as add-ons on those plans, without an add-on price in the readable pricing table. Enterprise pricing was custom. Check its current page and checkout before choosing.

[ContHunt pricing](/pricing) listed Pro at $25 per month billed annually ($300 per year), with MCP access, on the same date. The billing periods and included usage differ, so these figures are not a like-for-like per-video cost comparison. Check the current plan and allowances for your own workload.

## Which alternative answers the question?

| Option | Best starting point | Limit to remember |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shortimize | Ongoing tracking of public short-form accounts and videos | Check storage, refresh and add-on costs for the plan |
| YouTube Studio and Instagram Insights | First-party performance for accounts you manage | They do not reveal another account's private analytics |
| ContHunt | Public video discovery and creative research on TikTok, Reels and Shorts | Tracking is experimental; it is not a substitute for a mature tracking workflow |

Shortimize's [supported-platform guide](https://features.shortimize.com/en/help/articles/2674205-what-platforms-accounts-and-content-types-does-shortimize-support) describes public account tracking across several services. Its [storage guide](https://help.shortimize.com/articles/6026994-how-video-tracking-storage-works) defines storage as the number of videos actively tracked at once. A team whose main requirement is reliable recurring account tracking should test that workflow and its limits directly.

ContHunt helps when the decision comes earlier: which public examples should a team watch, and what hooks, topics or formats appear in them? Its Deep Search returns videos as evidence for the research question. Public observations do not give you a competitor's private retention or revenue. Use your platform's own analytics to evaluate your published work.

## Can an AI agent use the research?

ContHunt provides a hosted MCP server for supported AI clients. The [setup guide](/resources/using-conthunt-with-ai-agents-through-mcp) covers sign-in, example prompts and the distinction between reading saved work and starting new searches. Specify the platforms, date range and research budget before asking an agent to search.

Shortimize lists MCP as an option on Launch and Scale and as included in Enterprise. ContHunt includes its hosted [MCP integration](/docs/integrations/mcp) on every paid plan with no add-on fee. Ask each vendor for current metering terms and test the exact tools you need; the labels alone do not establish that two products expose the same endpoints.

## How should you compare the options?

1. Write the decision: a recurring tracking report, a creative brief, or performance analysis for your own account.
2. Use the same accounts, dates and examples in each trial. Check coverage and refresh where they matter.
3. Separate public counts from private audience and retention data, and separate research findings from measured outcomes.
4. Compare total cost, seats, billing terms and the usage your workflow consumes.
5. Keep the product that answers the question; use both research and tracking tools if each earns its place.
